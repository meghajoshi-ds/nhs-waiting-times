Oral Surgery waiting times in North West

Data: NHS England RTT · last updated March 2025

As of March 2025, the median wait to start oral surgery treatment in North West is about 21 weeks across 13 NHS providers. The fastest is Mersey And West Lancashire Teaching Hospitals Nhs Trust at about 10 weeks — roughly 46% below the national average. Choosing the fastest provider over the North West average could save around 11 weeks. You have a legal right to choose your provider for most planned care.

Frequently asked questions

What is the average oral surgery waiting time in North West?

The median wait is about 21 weeks as of March 2025, meaning half of patients waited less and half waited longer. This is the patient-weighted average of provider medians from NHS England RTT data.

Which North West provider has the shortest oral surgery wait?

Mersey And West Lancashire Teaching Hospitals Nhs Trust has the shortest median wait at about 10 weeks — around 46% below the national average.

Can I choose which hospital treats me?

For most planned (non-urgent) treatment in England you have a legal right to choose your provider, including one with a shorter wait. Ask your GP to refer you to the provider you prefer.

Why might my actual wait be different?

Median figures describe the whole waiting list. Your wait depends on your specific condition, clinical urgency and referral date, so individual waits vary widely around the median.

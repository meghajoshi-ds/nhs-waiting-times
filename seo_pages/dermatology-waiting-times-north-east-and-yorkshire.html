Dermatology waiting times in North East and Yorkshire

Data: NHS England RTT · last updated March 2025

As of March 2025, the median wait to start dermatology treatment in North East and Yorkshire is about 13 weeks across 15 NHS providers. The fastest is Mid Yorkshire Teaching Nhs Trust at about 4 weeks — roughly 71% below the national average. Choosing the fastest provider over the North East and Yorkshire average could save around 9 weeks. You have a legal right to choose your provider for most planned care.

Frequently asked questions

What is the average dermatology waiting time in North East and Yorkshire?

The median wait is about 13 weeks as of March 2025, meaning half of patients waited less and half waited longer. This is the patient-weighted average of provider medians from NHS England RTT data.

Which North East and Yorkshire provider has the shortest dermatology wait?

Mid Yorkshire Teaching Nhs Trust has the shortest median wait at about 4 weeks — around 71% below the national average.

Can I choose which hospital treats me?

For most planned (non-urgent) treatment in England you have a legal right to choose your provider, including one with a shorter wait. Ask your GP to refer you to the provider you prefer.

Why might my actual wait be different?

Median figures describe the whole waiting list. Your wait depends on your specific condition, clinical urgency and referral date, so individual waits vary widely around the median.

General Surgery waiting times in East of England

Data: NHS England RTT · last updated March 2025

As of March 2025, the median wait to start general surgery treatment in East of England is about 18 weeks across 13 NHS providers. The fastest is East And North Hertfordshire Nhs Trust at about 13 weeks — roughly 16% below the national average. Choosing the fastest provider over the East of England average could save around 5 weeks. You have a legal right to choose your provider for most planned care.

Frequently asked questions

What is the average general surgery waiting time in East of England?

The median wait is about 18 weeks as of March 2025, meaning half of patients waited less and half waited longer. This is the patient-weighted average of provider medians from NHS England RTT data.

Which East of England provider has the shortest general surgery wait?

East And North Hertfordshire Nhs Trust has the shortest median wait at about 13 weeks — around 16% below the national average.

Can I choose which hospital treats me?

For most planned (non-urgent) treatment in England you have a legal right to choose your provider, including one with a shorter wait. Ask your GP to refer you to the provider you prefer.

Why might my actual wait be different?

Median figures describe the whole waiting list. Your wait depends on your specific condition, clinical urgency and referral date, so individual waits vary widely around the median.

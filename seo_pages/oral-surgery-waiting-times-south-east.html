Oral Surgery waiting times in South East

Data: NHS England RTT · last updated March 2025

As of March 2025, the median wait to start oral surgery treatment in South East is about 21 weeks across 15 NHS providers. The fastest is Royal Berkshire Nhs Foundation Trust at about 10 weeks — roughly 45% below the national average. Choosing the fastest provider over the South East average could save around 11 weeks. You have a legal right to choose your provider for most planned care.

Frequently asked questions

What is the average oral surgery waiting time in South East?

The median wait is about 21 weeks as of March 2025, meaning half of patients waited less and half waited longer. This is the patient-weighted average of provider medians from NHS England RTT data.

Which South East provider has the shortest oral surgery wait?

Royal Berkshire Nhs Foundation Trust has the shortest median wait at about 10 weeks — around 45% below the national average.

Can I choose which hospital treats me?

For most planned (non-urgent) treatment in England you have a legal right to choose your provider, including one with a shorter wait. Ask your GP to refer you to the provider you prefer.

Why might my actual wait be different?

Median figures describe the whole waiting list. Your wait depends on your specific condition, clinical urgency and referral date, so individual waits vary widely around the median.

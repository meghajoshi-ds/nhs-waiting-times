Dermatology waiting times in England

Data: NHS England RTT · last updated March 2025

As of March 2025, the median wait to start dermatology treatment in England is about 15 weeks across 103 NHS providers. The fastest is Milton Keynes University Hospital Nhs Foundation Trust at about 2 weeks — roughly 86% below the national average. Choosing the fastest provider over the England average could save around 13 weeks. You have a legal right to choose your provider for most planned care.

Frequently asked questions

What is the average dermatology waiting time in England?

The median wait is about 15 weeks as of March 2025, meaning half of patients waited less and half waited longer. This is the patient-weighted average of provider medians from NHS England RTT data.

Which England provider has the shortest dermatology wait?

Milton Keynes University Hospital Nhs Foundation Trust has the shortest median wait at about 2 weeks — around 86% below the national average.

Can I choose which hospital treats me?

For most planned (non-urgent) treatment in England you have a legal right to choose your provider, including one with a shorter wait. Ask your GP to refer you to the provider you prefer.

Why might my actual wait be different?

Median figures describe the whole waiting list. Your wait depends on your specific condition, clinical urgency and referral date, so individual waits vary widely around the median.

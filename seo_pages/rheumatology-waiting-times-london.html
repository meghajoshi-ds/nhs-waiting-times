Rheumatology waiting times in London

Data: NHS England RTT · last updated March 2025

As of March 2025, the median wait to start rheumatology treatment in London is about 12 weeks across 16 NHS providers. The fastest is Guy'S And St Thomas' Nhs Foundation Trust at about 3 weeks — roughly 77% below the national average. Choosing the fastest provider over the London average could save around 9 weeks. You have a legal right to choose your provider for most planned care.

Frequently asked questions

What is the average rheumatology waiting time in London?

The median wait is about 12 weeks as of March 2025, meaning half of patients waited less and half waited longer. This is the patient-weighted average of provider medians from NHS England RTT data.

Which London provider has the shortest rheumatology wait?

Guy'S And St Thomas' Nhs Foundation Trust has the shortest median wait at about 3 weeks — around 77% below the national average.

Can I choose which hospital treats me?

For most planned (non-urgent) treatment in England you have a legal right to choose your provider, including one with a shorter wait. Ask your GP to refer you to the provider you prefer.

Why might my actual wait be different?

Median figures describe the whole waiting list. Your wait depends on your specific condition, clinical urgency and referral date, so individual waits vary widely around the median.
